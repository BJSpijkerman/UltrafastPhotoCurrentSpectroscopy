The triggers for the signal and reference arm of the box car averager are generated from the TTL signal of the laser. Due to the short nature of the pulse there is a high likely-hood that capturing the pulse corresponding to the original TTL signal is not possible, due to the response time of the electronics. We therefore choose to use the laser TTL signal to capture the "next pulse" $1\,\text{ms}$ later. The generation of the triggers is thus accomplished by transforming the incoming laser TTL into a $100\,\text{ns}$ window delayed by about a millisecond.
