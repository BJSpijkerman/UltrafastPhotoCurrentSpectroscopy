This product is part of a family of $2\,\text{A}$ synchronous step-down (buck) DC/DC converters. which takes a wide range of DC inputs (__$3.5\,\text{V}$ to $32\,\text{V}$__) and step it down to a lower regulated voltage. Specifically the __AP63203__ has a fixed $3.3\,\text{A}$ output making it ideal for powering the __ESP32__ powering the box car averager.

#### Pinouts & functions

| Pin | Name    | Function                                                                                                                                              | Wiring notes                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | __FB__  | __Feedback input__: Connects directly to $V_{\text{out}}$                                                                                             | --                                                                                                                        |
| 2   | __EN__  | __Enable input__: High-voltage digital on/off pin ($1.18\,\text{V}$ rising threshold).                                                                | Can be tied directly to $V_{\text{in}}$ or left floating for automatic startup via an internal $1.5\,\mu\text{A}$ pull-up |
| 3   | __VIN__ | __Power input__: $3.8\,\text{V}$ to $32\,\text{V}$ (DC abs max $40\,\text{V} for $400\,\text{ms}$$)                                                   | Requires a low equivalent series resistance by pass capacitor close to the pin.                                           |
| 4   | __GND__ | __Power ground__: Main ground reference and primary thermal path out of the IC.                                                                       |                                                                                                                           |
| 5   | __SW__  | __Switch node__: Connects to output inductor                                                                                                          |                                                                                                                           |
| 6   | __BST__ | __Bootstrap input__: Powers the high-side N-channel MOSFET gate driver via a $100\,\text{nF}$ ceramic capacitor connected between __BST__ and __SW__. |                                                                                                                           |
According to _Equation 7_ of the data sheet the output inductor value is calculated by:
$$
L=\frac{V_{\text{out}}\cdot\left(V_{\text{in}}-V_{\text{out}}\right)}{V_{\text{in}}\Delta I_L f_{\text{sw}}}
$$
