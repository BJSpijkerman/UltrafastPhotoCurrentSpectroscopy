The optical setup consists of a Mach-Zender interfermoter, light pulses enter the interferometer and are plit by a beam splitter and send down one of its two arm. The non-delayed arm is chopped at $500\,\text{Hz}$, removing every second laser pulse from this arm. The second arm hold a delay stage to impart a variable delay to the pulse. Both arms are combined on a second beamsplitter and are send colinearly onto the sample.
