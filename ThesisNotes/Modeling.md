In my current understanding of the project it seems most natural to use  a series of heat equations to describe the temperatures of the generated carriers and lattice, from where the conductivity can then be calculated,

$$
\nabla \left[\kappa(x, y)\nabla T_{el}(x, y)\right] - \gamma(x, y) C_{el}(x, y)\left[T_{ph}(x,y) - T_{el}(x, y)\right] + P(x, y)=0,
$$
$$
\nabla^2 T_{el}(x, y) - \gamma(x, y) C_{ph}(x, y)\left[T_{el}(x,y) - T_{ph}(x, y)\right]=0.
$$
or to fit the drude model,
$$
\sigma(\omega)=\frac{\omega_P^2\epsilon_0\tau}{1-i\omega\tau}.
$$
