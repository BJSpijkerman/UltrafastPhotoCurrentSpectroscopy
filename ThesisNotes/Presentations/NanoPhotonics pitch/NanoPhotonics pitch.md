# What is the goal
What we do is build a setup to perform photo- current/ conductivity measurements. This is a hybrid optical and electronic technique, where hot carriers are generated by laser pulses and the physical observable is the measured photocurrent or change in biased current/ voltage. This technique has an advantage over all optical methods in the probing of carrier dynamics due to the physical observable is only sensitive to charge generating and transport processes. The electrical detection also benefits from the excellent dynamic range and sensitivity of electrical detection techniques [[Ultrafast Spectroscopy with Photocurrent detection]]. It is however not suitable for samples that have low photocurrent generation or resistivity (as we found out with gold).
Photo current/ conductivity spectroscopy has many different variations, but the version that we will use it called correlation photo current/ conductivity spectroscopy. This is a variation of the base technique where $2$ inreferometrically delayed pulses are used to generate a non-linear photocurrent or conductivity change. The setup thus consists of a Mach-Zehnder interferometer by which two light pulses are seperated. One of the interfermeter arms is then chopped at $500\,\text{Hz}$ to create either $1$ or $2$ pulses incident on the sample every $2\,\text{ms}$. The resulting part of the conductivity change can then be written as:
$$
\Delta\sigma=\Delta\sigma_1+\Delta\sigma_2+\Delta\Sigma_{1,2},
$$
where $\Delta\sigma_1$ and $\Delta\sigma_2$ are the conductivity changes induced by pulses $1$ and $2$ independently and $\Delta\Sigma_{1,2}$ is the non-linear reaction when both pulses are present. By chopping the beam we measure $\Delta\sigma$ and $\Delta\sigma_1$, and by taking a reference every so often we can obtain $\Delta\sigma_2$. The non-linear part relative to the linear reaction to the second pulse can then be obtained by the calculation:
$$
\frac{\Delta\Sigma_{1,2}}{\Delta\sigma_2}=\frac{\Delta\sigma-\Delta\sigma_1}{\Delta\sigma_2}
$$
This non-linear change in conductivity is said to contain information about carrier-carrier interaction and transport phenomena. The electrical detection of this quantity is however a challenge as  most papers use a lock-in amplifier, however due to the very short signal duration we are afraid that the signal will disappear in noise. Therefore we consider using gating to sample-and-hold the integrated peak value.......
