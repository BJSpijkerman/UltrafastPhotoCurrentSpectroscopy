#### Summary of last week
Last week I finished the code to measure with the setup. The stage now moves and waits for the lock in to measure and then moves again all settings are adjusted correctly and everything is warped inside a GUI.

After mounting the new stage on _Thursday 6-11-2026_ using the small mounting plate _Hanwen_ and I found that is bends when the carriage is not above the mounting plate. Thus we removed the mounting plate and tried to align again the day after. There we found that when moving the carriage over the length of the stage the side closest to the sample dips by about $300\,\mu\text{m}$ giving a deflection in the height of the laser beam of about $2.5\,\text{mm}$ over a distance of $165\,\text{cm}$ This is far to much. After asking _Jan Bonne Aans_ for advice we settled on designing a new mounting plate for the stage image shown below.
![[Screenshot_20261110_085749.png]]
On the bottom it has three feet to make it stable. Currently I am working in tandem to estimate the bending due to the weight of the mirrors for refining the placement of the feet and generating the mechanical drawings from the 3D design. I would like to finish this on Friday this week and also get started on making the power supply circuit for the [[Box car arm]]

### Further progress
I have made some small progress by constructing a FEM simulation in the Python library Dolfinx to simulate the bending of the mounting plate under some load. At current dimensions a load of $2.2$ metric tons placed at the center produces a maximum deflection of about $900\,\mu\text{m}$ assuming this deflection takes the same shape as calculated in [[Experimental Setup/Stage Mounting Plate]] we can calculate the maximum deflection of the laser spot on the wall.
