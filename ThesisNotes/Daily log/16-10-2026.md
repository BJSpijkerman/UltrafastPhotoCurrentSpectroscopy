# Electrical detection meeting (with Ioannis)
In this meeting we discussed the electrical detection strategy with Ioannis and decided to use an adaptation of the THz box car averager from Ioannis to try and measure our signal.
![[CIRCUITS FOR UPC_1.pdf]]
The strategy described below is made due to the fear that the signal will arrive at the circuit between the trigger and the response of the trigger IC. This will cause us to always miss the signal when trying to measure. In response to this we want to use the TTL trigger of every pulse to acquire the next pulse.
![[CIRCUITS FOR UPC_2.pdf]]
We use two IC's to generate set and reset signals spaces by approximately $100\,\text{ns}$ around the arrival of the next laser TTL pulse to capture the signal with a pulse integrator and and an ADC. The ADC is controlled by a microcontroler "ESP32" and read out by a laptop.
