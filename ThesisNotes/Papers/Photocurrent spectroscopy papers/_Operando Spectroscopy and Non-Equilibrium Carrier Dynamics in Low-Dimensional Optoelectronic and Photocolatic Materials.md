## Future perspectives & Open Challenges
- Bridging the Operational gap: How to transition from ultrafast pulsed optical control (High fluence requirement) to continuous-wave (1-sun) solar conditions.
- Coupling Thermal and Electronic Engineering: Utilizing phononic bottlenecks (e.g. in MXenes or perovskite superlattices) to drive next-generation photothermal electronics and hot-carriers photovoltaics
- Automated & AI-driven Operando Characterization: Combining techniques like AI-TA with automated synthesis to perform real-time multi-dimensional material optimization in minutes instead of months
