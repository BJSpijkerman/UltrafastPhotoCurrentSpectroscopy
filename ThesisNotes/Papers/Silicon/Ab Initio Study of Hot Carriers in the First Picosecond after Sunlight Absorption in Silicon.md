In this work the [[hot carrier dynamics]] of electron hole pairs in silicon is discussed from a theoretical point of view.

# Key takeaways
- __Dominant Mechanism__: Hot carrier thermalization in silicon under solar illumination is dominated by phonon emission processes rather than electron-electron scattering.
- __Time Scale__: Initial hot carriers generated by AM1.5 sunlight complete thermalization withing \~$350\,\text{fs}$.  _Is this electron thermalization of electron-lattice thermalization?_
- __Relaxation Dyncamics__: Relaxation is fast (~$10-20\,\text{fs}$) at high excess energies away from the band edges, but slows significantly to $50-100\,\text{fs}$ within $300\,\text{meV}$ of the band edges, making the near-band-edge region the main bottleneck for overall thermalization.
- __Mean Free Paths (MFPs)__:
	- __Hot  electrons__: MFPs are mostly under $5\,\text{nm}$, except along the $\[100\] direction near the conduction band minimum (CBM), where they reach up to $15\,\text{nm}$.
	- __Hot holes__: MFPs reach up to $15\,\text{nm}$ and are isotropic across all principal crystallographic directions (\[100\], \[110\], \[111\]).

# Methodology
The authors developed a parameter-free, ab initio theoretical framework combining:
- __Density Functional Theory (DFT)__: withing the local density approximation (LDA) for ground-state electronic and lattice properties.
- __Density Functional Perturbation Theory (DFPT)__: combined with __Wannier interpolation (EPW)__ on fine Brillouin Zone (BZ) grids ($50\times50\times50$) to calculate electron-phonon (e-ph) matrix elements and self-energies.
- __Many-Body Perturbation Theory ($G_0W_0$)__: using BerkeleyGW to evaluate electron-electron (e-e) self-energies.
- __Boltzmann Transport Equation__: in the relaxation-time approximation to step forward the non-equilibrium carrier occupations $f_{n,k}(t)$.

# Findings
### 1. Electron-Phonon vs. Electron-Electron Scattering
- $Im\left(\Sigma^{e-ph}\right)$ is significantly larger than $Im\left(\Sigma^{e-e}\right)$  across the solar excitation energy spectrum.
- _e-e_ scattering rates are negligible within $3\,\text{eV}$ of the mid-gap and vanish within one bandgap ($R_{\text{g}}=1.15\,\text{eV}$) of the valance band maximum (VBM) and CBM, as zero temperature _GW_ calculation only capture interband impact ionization/Auger processes.
- Because low photo-generated carrier densities under solar illumination (\~$10^{17}\,\text{cm}^{-3}$) minimize interband _e-e_ interactions, __phonon emission is the primary driver of thermalization__.

### 2. Energy and Momentum ($k$) Dependence
- Relaxation rates mirror the electronic density of states (DOS), as DOS regulates available scattering phase space.
- Specific momentum-dependent features, such as lower scattering rates around the L valley (~$1\,\text{eV}$ above the CBM).
- Multiple overlapping bands at higher energies provide more phonon emission channels, accelerating relaxation times down to \~$10\,\text{fs}$.

### 3. Solar Thermallization Simulation & Comparison to Experiment
- Under AM1.5 solar irradiation, carriers generated $0.8\,\text{eV}$ above the CBM lose \~$60\,\text{meV}$ per optical phonon every \~$10fs$ (losing $600\,\text{meV}$ in $100\,\text{fs}$) until reaching within $200\,\text{meV}$ of the band edge, where relazation slows to $250\,\text{fs}$.
- The calculated total thermalization time of $\approx350\,\text{fs}$ directly matches time-resolved optical pump-probe reflectivity experiments by Doany & Grischekowsky.

### 4. Design Guidelines for Hot Carrier Collection
To extract hot carriers before they thermalize (relaxing within distances shorter than their MFP):
- __Hot Electrons__: Best collected in thin films of __Si(100)__ with thicknesses $<10\,\text{nm}$ using excitation energies around $1.5\,\text{eV}$.
- __Hot Holes__: Can be extracted from thin films up to $15\,\text{nm}$ thick with any crystal orientation for excitation energies in the $1.2-3\,\text{eV}$ range.
